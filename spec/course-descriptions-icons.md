## Design notes

- Use the same icon for courses with similar topics to make the catalog easier to scan; show the online format with a separate label.
- Icon names and availability may differ between Iconsax packages or versions. Check the library used by your project before finalizing the design.
- The **HRDF Claimable** wording, TÜV Rheinland names, dates, and course formats are preserved from the reference; their status and claims should be confirmed.
